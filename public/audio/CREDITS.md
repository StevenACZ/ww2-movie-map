# Sound credits

All sounds are recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). Files are trimmed, faded and level-adjusted for the site. Most are mono; the Dunkirk surf retains stereo. The Dunkirk surf and launch use two-second equal-power crossfades with 0.2-second padding around their 14-second loop regions. The Spitfire flyby is a mono excerpt with fades, filtering and gain adjustment; its stereo movement is applied during playback.
